Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and tracking services.

How fast will a technician be assigned to my request?
Our automated dispatch engine matches qualified local technicians based on proximity and skill availability. On average, a technician accepts and is assigned to your request within 2 to 5 minutes.
How are technicians background checked and verified?
All technicians registered on Seva Bandhu undergo identity verification, police background verification, and technical skill screening before they are authorized to accept customer requests.
What payment methods are supported?
We support online digital payments (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking) as well as offline payment directly to the technician upon job completion. Digital PDF receipts are generated instantly for all transactions.
Can I track my technician's live location?
Yes! Once a technician accepts your service request and starts navigation, you can track their en-route progress live on your customer tracking dashboard.
How do I join Seva Bandhu as a skilled service technician?
Click on "Join as Technician" on the home page or navigate to Technician Signup, fill out your profile details and skill categories, complete verification, and start receiving job requests nearby!
